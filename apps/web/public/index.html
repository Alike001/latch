THE PRODUCT

Recovery is a market transaction, not a blind fallback.

Latch only asks Telegraph when the machine’s required local intelligence is unavailable or invalid. Every returned signal has to survive the Evidence Gate before it can influence the task.

01

Detect

Local telemetry is stale, unavailable, semantically wrong, or fails integrity policy.

02

Authorize

Check revocation, per-purchase ceiling, attempt limit, and session spend cap.

03

Acquire

Buy the missing telemetry through Telegraph from an independent Provider.

04

Verify

Validate identity, metric, unit, value, time, freshness, plausibility, and integrity.

05

Decide

CONTINUE, REBUY, HOLD, or ESCALATE. HTTP 200 alone is never permission to act.
