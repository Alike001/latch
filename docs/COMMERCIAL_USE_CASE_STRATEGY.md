# Commercial Use Case Strategy — 2026-11-25

## Source and status

This record preserves guidance shared by a Telegraph administrator in the
Season II Commercial Use Cases Discord thread on 2026-11-25. It is
user-supplied evidence, not an independently verified rule change.

The administrator advised builders to:

- prioritize the new Tencent, ByteDance and Virtuals opportunities;
- finish existing use cases before taking an additional unclaimed one;
- build Apps & Agents when prior work focused on Miners or Evaluators;
- reuse existing products, Miners and Evaluators instead of starting over;
- allow one strong application to address multiple ecosystem use cases;
- demonstrate working functionality, real Telegraph intelligence consumption,
  receipts, independently sourced evidence and measurable results;
- state the commercial customer, reason to pay and recurring-revenue path;
- label sandbox and testnet behavior clearly;
- provide a repository, walkthrough, results and live demonstration;
- think beyond the hackathon toward products, users and recurring revenue.

The administrator directly advised the Latch builder to focus on these Apps,
Agents and integration opportunities for the highest chance of winning and of
turning the project into a product, while separately investigating the
Evaluator query.

## Evidence impact

This guidance does not invalidate Mission 11 Route 2. It changes prioritization:

1. Finish and submit the working Latch Apps & Agents and Miner contributions.
2. Keep the failed Evaluator route paused; do not retrain or re-register without
   new evidence and the existing quality gates.
3. Make the current app demonstrable, truthful and commercially legible.
4. Only then claim one adjacent commercial opportunity that reuses the same
   purchase, budget, receipt, verification and audit capabilities.

The reusable product statement is:

> Latch is an intelligence-assurance layer for autonomous systems. It purchases
> missing specialist intelligence through Telegraph, validates the evidence,
> and records the resulting autonomous decision.

The cold-chain machine remains the first concrete deployment. This broader
statement must not turn Latch into a generic chatbot or erase its telemetry
market origin.

## Opportunity assessment

### 1. Virtuals — Telegraph Intelligence Marketplace for ACP

**Fit: strongest adjacent opportunity.**

Reusable Latch capabilities:

- bounded intelligence purchasing;
- Provider discovery/pinning boundary;
- payment and receipt evidence;
- deterministic result verification;
- completed-result and audit records;
- real Telegraph testnet proof.

Required new work before claiming it:

- confirm the use case is still unclaimed and obtain its thread number;
- integrate the actual Virtuals Agent Commerce Protocol;
- execute a real ACP job whose intelligence is purchased through Telegraph;
- preserve ACP and Telegraph payment/result evidence separately;
- document installation and reproduction steps.

Do not claim Virtuals or ACP compatibility until this integration runs.

### 2. Tencent — Independent Enterprise Agent Verification

**Fit: conceptually strong, larger implementation gap.**

The Evidence Gate and missing-intelligence recovery pattern are reusable, but a
qualifying entry requires actual Tencent Cloud ADP or Hunyuan use across several
enterprise tasks. This is a future extension, not current functionality.

### 3. Tencent — Enterprise Decision Assurance

**Fit: commercially strong, but would require a new decision domain.**

Latch's assurance loop fits supplier or counterparty decisions, but a real
Hunyuan-powered business case, source-backed specialist intelligence and a
materially changed decision are not yet implemented.

### ByteDance opportunities

**Fit: weak for the current deadline.** Creative generation and visual quality
assurance would reuse little of the telemetry Provider and would risk producing
an unrelated second application.

## Decision

- Current committed work: polish, verify and submit Latch for Apps & Agents and
  Miner/Provider.
- Adjacent commercial target: Virtuals — Telegraph Intelligence Marketplace for
  ACP, gated by claim availability and a real ACP integration.
- Evaluator: preserve evidence and await administrator guidance; no blind retry.
- UI: use the Instrument Panel direction so a judge can understand the complete
  failure-to-decision loop in under 90 seconds.

## Claim gate

Before replying in the Commercial Use Cases thread:

1. obtain the actual numeric use case identifier;
2. verify it remains unclaimed;
3. confirm there is enough time after Latch submission readiness;
4. post a narrow extension statement, not a promise to build a separate product;
5. list the ACP integration or access needed from the team.
